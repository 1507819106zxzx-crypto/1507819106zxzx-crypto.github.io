Whelpbook Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-16. Developer: Arcane Pocket Games. Contact: 1507819106zxzx@gmail.com

Summary

The developer does not receive or sell the records you enter in this app. Google Play handles purchases as described below. Your kennel records stay on your device.

Data stored on your device

Dogs, litters, puppy weights, health tasks, temperatures, notes, puppy photos and buyer details (names, phone numbers, email addresses, deposits) you enter are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. The developer never receives them.

Data that leaves your device

Only files you choose to export or share (puppy-packet PDFs and backup files) leave the app, and only to the destination you pick. If you use Android's device backup, Android may back up app data to your Google account under Google's terms.

Purchases

The optional Whelpbook Pro upgrade is sold through Google Play. Payment is processed by Google; the app only learns whether Pro is owned.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, Whelpbook schedules local notifications on your device for weigh-ins and due health tasks. Nothing is sent over the internet.

Buyer information

You are responsible for the buyer details you choose to record and for honouring their privacy. Delete a buyer in the app to remove their details.

No tracking

Whelpbook contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software and does not use the advertising identifier.

Children

Whelpbook is a record-keeping tool for adult dog breeders and is not directed at children.

Deleting your data

Delete items inside the app, or uninstall the app to remove all of its data from your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
