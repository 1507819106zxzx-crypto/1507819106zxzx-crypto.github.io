What is free?

One active litter with unlimited puppies, two breeding dogs, three buyers and backups.

Optional Pro is planned as a one-time US$14.99 purchase, with no subscription or ads. The final local price will be shown in Google Play. Testing the free features does not require a purchase.

A useful test

Use a fictional litter to record births and two weights, edit a task, export a backup and check saved records after reopening. Feedback is about record keeping, not veterinary advice.
