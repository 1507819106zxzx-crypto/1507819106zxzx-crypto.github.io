BibClock: Race Timer & Results

Offline finish-line timing and results for small races.

Google Play closed test is open — version 1.0.1. Verified 18 October 2026. Join the testers-community group (or google-play-test-users) with the same Google account you use in Play. Then become a tester and open the Google Play listing. Android 8.0 or later. This is an early closed test, not the production release.

What is free?

Unlimited races and recording; results and CSV show the first 25 finishers.

Optional Pro is planned as a one-time US$24.99 purchase, with no subscription or ads. The final local price will be shown in Google Play. Testing the free features does not require a purchase.

A useful test

Create a practice race with fictional bib numbers, record a few finishes, export a free CSV, then reopen the app and check the results.
