BibClock Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-16. Developer: Arcane Pocket Games. Contact: 1507819106zxzx@gmail.com

Summary

The developer does not receive or sell the records you enter in this app. Google Play handles purchases as described below. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Data stored on your device

Race details you enter — race names, participant names, bib numbers, ages, genders, teams and finish times — are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. The developer never receives this data.

Data that leaves your device

Only files you choose to share or export (for example a results CSV, PDF, web page or backup file) leave the app, and only to the destination you pick. If you use Android's device backup, Android may back up app data to your Google account under Google's terms.

Purchases

The optional BibClock Pro upgrade is sold through Google Play. Payment is processed by Google; the app only learns whether Pro is owned. See Google's privacy policy for how Google handles payment information.

No tracking

BibClock contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software and does not use the advertising ID.

Children

BibClock is a tool for race organisers and is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Delete a race inside the app, or uninstall the app to remove all of its data from your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
