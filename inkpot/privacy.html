Inkpot Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-16. Developer: Arcane Pocket Games. Contact: 1507819106zxzx@gmail.com

Summary

The developer does not receive or sell the records you enter in this app. Google Play handles purchases as described below. Your homeschool records stay on your device.

Data stored on your device

Student names and grades, subjects, school-year targets, logged lessons, notes, attendance marks, work-sample photos and your school details are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. Inkpot also keeps automatic daily copies of your records for the last 14 days in the same private storage. The developer never receives any of it.

Photos

Work-sample photos are chosen through the Android photo picker. Inkpot copies a resized version of each chosen photo into its private storage. It has no camera or storage permission and cannot see other photos.

Data that leaves your device

Only files you choose to export or share (PDF reports, portfolio PDFs, CSV logs and backup files) leave the app, and only to the destination you pick. If you use Android's device backup, Android may back up the records database and settings to your Google account under Google's terms; a direct transfer to a new phone may also copy photos.

Purchases

The optional Inkpot Pro upgrade is sold through Google Play. Payment is processed by Google; the app only learns whether Pro is owned.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, Inkpot schedules a local notification on your device. Nothing is sent over the internet.

No tracking

Inkpot contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software and does not use the advertising identifier.

Children

Inkpot is a record-keeping tool for parents and teachers and is not directed at children. Information about children is entered by the parent and never leaves the device unless the parent shares a file.

Deleting your data

Delete entries inside the app, or uninstall the app to remove all of its data, photos and snapshots from your device. Backup files you saved elsewhere are under your control.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
