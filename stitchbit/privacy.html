Stitchbit Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-16. Developer: Arcane Pocket Games. Contact: 1507819106zxzx@gmail.com

Summary

The developer does not receive or sell the records you enter in this app. Google Play handles purchases as described below. Your graphs and photos stay on your device.

Data stored on your device

Graphs, colour names, yarn settings and tracking progress you create are stored only in the app's private storage on your device.

Data that leaves your device

Only files you choose to share or export (chart images, PDF or text patterns, project files) leave the app, and only to the destination you pick. If you use Android's device backup, Android may back up app data to your Google account under Google's terms.

Purchases

The optional Stitchbit Pro upgrade is sold through Google Play. Payment is processed by Google; the app only learns whether Pro is owned.

Photos

When you import a photo, you choose it with the Android photo picker. Stitchbit reads that one picture on your device to build a graph and keeps only the resulting graph. Photos are never uploaded.

No tracking

Stitchbit contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software and does not use the advertising identifier.

Children

Stitchbit is a craft tool for teens and adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Delete items inside the app, or uninstall the app to remove all of its data from your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
