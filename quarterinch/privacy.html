Quarter Inch Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-16. Developer: Arcane Pocket Games. Contact: 1507819106zxzx@gmail.com

Summary

Quarter Inch does not collect, transmit or sell personal data. Your quilt plans stay on your device.

Data stored on your device

Quilt plans (names, blocks, fabrics, colours and measurements) and your settings are stored only in the app's private storage. The developer never receives them.

Data that leaves your device

Only files you choose to share (PDF patterns and backup files) or text you copy (shopping lists) leave the app, and only to the destination you pick. If you use Android's device backup, Android may back up app data to your Google account under Google's terms.

Purchases

The optional Quarter Inch Pro upgrade is sold through Google Play. Payment is processed by Google; the app only learns whether Pro is owned.

Permissions

Quarter Inch asks for no permissions beyond what Google Play Billing needs. It does not use the camera, photos, location or contacts.

No tracking

Quarter Inch contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software and does not use the advertising identifier.

Children

Quarter Inch is a planning tool for adult quilters and is not directed at children.

Deleting your data

Delete quilts inside the app, or uninstall the app to remove all of its data from your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
