What is free?

One quilt with all calculations, cutting lists and shopping lists.

A useful test

Plan a simple Nine Patch quilt, compare dimensions and yardage with a pattern you may use, note any setting differences, copy the shopping list and reopen. Treat estimates as test output; do not buy or cut fabric from an unverified estimate.
